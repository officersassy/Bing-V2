V2.4 GENERAL SASSY VOICE ANNOUNCER

Based on stable V2.3.17.

Voice events:
- Game start: random 1 of 5
- PD pause: random 1 of 5
- PD resume: random 1 of 5
- Invalid Bingo: random 1 of 6 on claimant device
- Slow game: random 1 of 4, once per round (45 calls on 75-ball / 55 calls on 90-ball)
- Random mid-game sass: random 1 of 6, low chance and at least 7 calls apart

Winner voice clips are intentionally NOT included. Existing Celebration winner music is unchanged.
The fullhouse-02 test recording is intentionally excluded.

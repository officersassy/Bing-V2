Kick Loop Fix

Problem:
The kick record stayed in Firebase after the player was removed.
When the same account loaded player.html again, the old kick fired again,
creating an endless redirect/kick loop.

Fix:
- The player sees the kick message once.
- Their own kick record is deleted as an acknowledgement.
- They are signed out.
- They return to index.html cleanly.
- If they later sign back in, the old kick does not fire again.
- Players can ONLY delete their own kick record; they cannot create or edit kicks.
